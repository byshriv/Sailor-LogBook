# Community conduct

Be respectful, constructive and specific. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing,
spam and publishing someone else's private logbook data or credentials are unacceptable.
Discuss the work and accommodate different experience levels and accessibility needs.

The maintainer may remove harmful content, close disruptive conversations and restrict
participation. Ordinary moderation concerns can be raised in a repository issue once the
repository exists, using no sensitive details. A private conduct contact has not yet been
published; do not assume that a private reporting channel exists.
